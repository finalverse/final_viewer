# Phase 4C — Finalverse growth loop (prepared, deferred)

Updated 2026-10-08. Do not execute until 4B visual/avatar/semantic/performance/regression gates pass, 4B.1 starter provisioning is reliable, and 3–5 genuinely new users complete the five-minute study with its major findings addressed.

Build CREATE → INVITE → SHARE → JOIN → REMIX → RETURN over the MutSea platform. Finalverse supplies the consumer interface; MutSea remains authoritative. Reuse mature social/presence/permissions and the Phase 4A facade. Preserve Harbor, Lumi identity/history and starter package versions.

Deliver versioned secure world/location/entity HTTPS and Finalverse deep links; copy-link/QR sharing; destination landing and install/open/resume; narrow public registration with uniqueness, secure credentials, reset, rate limits and abuse controls; recoverable starter avatar/body/outfit/inventory provisioning that reaches ready before entry; inviter presence and destination context; independent owned remixes with rights and provenance; privacy-conscious funnel telemetry; block/mute/report; and monitored staging deployment with TLS, DNS, networking, persistence, backups and rollback.

An invitation grants bounded access, not ownership or edit/admin authority. Tokens are unguessable, optionally expiring and revocable. Links contain no credentials or arbitrary commands. Package provenance and no-copy/remix rights remain enforced. All AI edits/remixes still require grounding, policy, preview, explicit authorization, deterministic transactions and WorldLine. Never expose the simulator operator/admin console as registration.

Instrument invite/open/install/register/world entry/destination/Lumi/create/share/remix and D1/D7 return without logging conversation contents by default. Measure conversion and real user behavior; developer-only smoke tests do not prove growth.

Canonical acceptance: Alice creates and shares a garden; Bob opens the link, registers with a provisioned visible avatar, arrives at the garden, sees Alice/Lumi, creates an independently owned permitted remix, reviews an AI modification, applies it through policy, sees correct provenance and shares again. Verify privacy, abuse controls, social safety, restart and Phase 4A/3 regressions.

Defer economy, payouts, crypto, advertising, recommendation feeds and mobile apps. After real growth validation, Phase 5 expands Community Platform, Templates, WorldSync/Blender Live, Creator Hub, Third-Party SDK, Spatial Commerce, Enterprise and Creator Economy. Do not relabel those planned capabilities as existing services.
